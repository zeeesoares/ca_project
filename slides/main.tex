\section{Introduction}

\subsection{Context}
\begin{frame}{Context}
    LLM training spans several weeks, requiring periodic checkpointing to a Parallel File System (PFS) to prevent total state loss during node failures.
    
    \vspace{4ex}
    However, checkpointing directly to the PFS creates two problems:
    \begin{itemize}
        \small
        \item Simultaneous writes create heavy I/O contention, causing the "noisy neighbor" problem for other jobs.
        \item High write latency stalls training, leading to significant compute time being wasted.
    \end{itemize}

\end{frame}

\subsection{Problem}
\begin{frame}{Problem}
    \centering
    \small
    \begin{tabular}{l p{0.6\textwidth}}
        \hline
        \textbf{Challenge} & \textbf{Impact} \\ \hline
        \noalign{\smallskip}
        PFS Contention & I/O bottlenecks slow training as nodes compete for bandwidth. \\ 
        \noalign{\smallskip}
        Resilience & Hardware failures at scale risk losing weeks of training progress. \\ 
        \noalign{\smallskip}
        Checkpointing & Larger models $\rightarrow$ larger checkpoints $\rightarrow$ higher storage costs/time. \\ 
        \noalign{\smallskip}
        Storage Costs & Frequent PFS writes increase costs and operational overhead. \\ \noalign{\smallskip}
        \hline
    \end{tabular}
    
    \vspace{1em}
    \footnotesize
    \textit{Table 1: Challenges of LLM Checkpointing to Parallel File Systems}
\end{frame}
